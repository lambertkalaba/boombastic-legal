Privacy Policy – Boombastic: Marble Shooter

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Boombastic: Marble Shooter (“the app”) is a game published by Lambert Kalaba Kamenga (“we”). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Your progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may use the device advertising identifier (IDFA) and device information (such as device model, OS version, IP address and ad interactions) to show and measure ads, and to show personalized ads if you allow tracking. On iOS you can choose whether the app may track you through the system prompt and at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can also opt out of personalized ads in the app settings where available. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (coin packs and “No ads”) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. Data processed by advertising partners is handled under their own policies. Game data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or another region with privacy laws, you may ask us about any data related to you. Since the app does not store personal data on our servers, there is normally nothing to delete, but you can contact us at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
